What this means for New York & New Jersey

What this means for New York

72 cases$133.7M exposure72.2% confirmed52.8% material weakness6 elevated-focus pair(s)

New York has 72 of the dataset's 118 cases and $133.7M of $210.0M in tracked exposure — the largest share of either. 72.2% are independently confirmed, and 69.4% are from the last 10 years.

38 cases (52.8%) reached Material Weakness severity, carrying 94.7% of NY's exposure. Bribery, Kickback & Authorization Controls and Payroll, Timekeeping & Certified-Payroll Controls are tied as the most common control failures (19 cases each). Payroll, Timekeeping & Certified-Payroll Controls carries the largest dollar exposure ($61.3M).

6 agency/category pairings clear the register's ‘Elevated Focus’ bar in NY — pervasive, at least one Material Weakness case, and still active: NYC SCA × Payroll (4 cases, $38.2M); MTA × MWBE/DBE & Professional-Certification Controls (6 cases, $32.3M); NYC DOE × Payroll (3 cases, $14.2M); NYCHA × Bribery (8 cases, $2.1M); NYSDOT × MWBE/DBE & Professional-Certification Controls (3 cases, $2.0M); DASNY × MWBE/DBE & Professional-Certification Controls (5 cases, $1.3M).

This describes where enforcement has concentrated historically — it is not a prediction that any specific project or agency will see fraud.

What this means for New Jersey

35 cases$41.7M exposure71.4% confirmed34.3% material weakness1 elevated-focus pair(s)

New Jersey has 35 of the 118 cases and $41.7M in tracked exposure. 71.4% are independently confirmed, but only 31.4% are from the last 10 years — a more historical caseload than New York's 69.4%.

Bribery and kickback control failures are the most common category by far (13 cases, 37% of NJ's total), from municipal-redevelopment and pay-to-play prosecutions. But the largest dollar exposure sits elsewhere, in disbursement and change-order failures at NJ school-construction contracts ($30.1M) — NJ's frequency risk and its dollar risk point to two different controls.

Only one NJ-linked pairing clears ‘Elevated Focus’: Port Authority (NY/NJ) × Bribery (6 cases, $10.2M) — the bi-state Port Authority pattern below. NJ Municipal Redevelopment × Bribery is the most pervasive NJ-specific pairing (7 cases), second only to NYCHA in NY (8 cases), but doesn't clear Elevated Focus: only 14% of its cases are recent — mostly the 2009-2016 Hudson County era, not an actively recurring pattern.

This describes where enforcement has concentrated historically — it is not a prediction that any specific project or agency will see fraud.

The bi-state exception: Port Authority of NY & NJ

The 11 cases coded NY/NJ are almost all Port Authority of New York and New Jersey matters. All are independently confirmed, and 90.9% are Material Weakness-tier — the highest concentration of confirmed, high-severity findings in the dataset. This is why Port Authority × Bribery appears as Elevated Focus in both state sections above.

Typology methodology

How the fraud-mechanism classification above was produced

Each case is tagged with a fraud-mechanism archetype, its concrete red flags, and the verification step that would have closed the gap — adapted from recent transportation-fraud research methods. This is precomputed once from each case's already-cited source, not generated live. Treat it as a hypothesis for review, not a verified finding.
